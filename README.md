# MediFinder

## Problem Statement

Finding medicines, comparing online purchasing options, and locating nearby pharmacies can be inconvenient because this information is often spread across different platforms. In urgent situations, people may struggle to identify nearby medical stores, find reliable medicine information, or determine where to purchase the required medicine. Managing regular medications and remembering dosage schedules can also be challenging.

For example, imagine a person whose family member needs a particular medicine late in the evening. They search multiple websites to find the medicine, but they cannot easily determine which nearby pharmacy might stock it. They must then search for medical stores separately, check their locations, and contact them individually. This process can waste valuable time, especially when the medicine is needed urgently.

## Proposed Solution

MediFinder is a healthcare web application designed to simplify medicine discovery, online purchasing, and pharmacy location services through a single platform. It enables users to search for medicines, explore product information, access available online purchasing links, and locate nearby pharmacies using map integration.

The platform can also provide personal medicine management features, including medication schedules, dosage reminders based on user-entered or prescribed instructions, and refill tracking. By bringing these services together, MediFinder aims to make finding medicines and accessing pharmacy information more convenient and organized.

### Example

Suppose Rahul needs a prescribed medicine for his father. Instead of searching multiple websites and manually looking for medical stores, Rahul opens MediFinder, searches for the medicine, checks the available purchasing options, and uses the pharmacy locator to find nearby medical stores. He can contact a pharmacy to confirm stock before travelling and save his father's medication schedule to help remember the prescribed doses.

In this way, MediFinder helps reduce the effort involved in finding medicines and pharmacies while providing a centralized, user-friendly healthcare information platform.

**Note:** Pharmacy listings and online product information do not guarantee real-time stock availability. Users should confirm availability with the pharmacy, and prescription medicines must be obtained in accordance with applicable requirements.
